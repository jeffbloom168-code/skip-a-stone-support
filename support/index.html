Restore a purchase

Open the Full Access screen in the app and choose “Restore Purchase.” Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase. Full Access is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Sound

Sound is part of the core experience and may play even when your iPhone’s Ring/Silent switch is set to silent. You can turn sound off from Settings inside the app.

Your scenes and progress

The app does not require an account. Preferences and app state are stored locally. Reinstalling the app may reset local settings, while eligible purchases can be restored through Apple.
