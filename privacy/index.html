Privacy Policy

“Skip a Stone?” is designed as a local-first experience. No account is required, and we do not use advertising or third-party analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app stores preferences and local state such as onboarding completion, sound settings, your selected scene, and purchase entitlement cache on your device. Deleting the app removes this local app data, subject to iOS behavior and any device backups you control.

Device motion

The app may use motion sensor input to create visual parallax. This motion input is used locally for the experience and is not transmitted or stored by us.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card information. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, and maintain your entitlement across eligible devices.

For those purposes, RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier, product and transaction information, entitlement status, and limited app, device, or diagnostic information needed to provide the purchase service. Learn more in RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or profile.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking.
  • We do not collect precise location data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
